How To Play Trackle

Guess the mystery Track + Distance Course (e.g. Niigata 2000m Outer or Hanshin 3200m) in 6 attempts! Each guess provides color-coded attribute feedback:
GREEN
Exact Match! Exact match on track, distance, distance type, surface, direction, or late race location.
YELLOW
Close / Partial! Same region, distance within ±200m, corner count within ±1, final straight within ±35m, or matching late race category (Corner vs Corner, or Straight vs Straight).
GRAY
Mismatch. Attribute does not match.
⬆️ / ⬇️
Direction Arrows: Indicate if the secret course has a higher (⬆️) or lower (⬇️) value for Distance, Corner Count, or Final Straight length!
DIST TYPE
Distance Type: Short (≤1400m), Mile (1401–1800m), Medium (1801–2400m), or Long (≥2401m). Strict Green / Gray only (no yellow).
TURN
Turn (Race Schedule): Half-month calendar period (e.g. Late December, Early May). Yellow means within ±2 turns (1 month). ⬆️ / ⬇️ indicates whether the race is scheduled later or earlier in the calendar year.
LATE RACE
Late Race Start (終盤入り): The critical 2/3 mark where acceleration skills activate: C2, C3, C4, Backstretch, or Homestretch. Yellow means both are corners or both are straights; Gray means corner vs straight.
